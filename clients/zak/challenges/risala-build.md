# Challenge — Building Risala

> **Risala** (رسالة, Arabic for "letter / message"). The active build-in-public challenge for this cycle. Monday announces it; Friday reports the week's output. Source of truth linking Mon → Fri posts.

## What it is
An **AI agent that writes newsletters** for creators ("creator-preneurs") and education companies — built by Zak under Sawa with co-founder Ziad Hassan.

## The vision (what makes Risala different)
- NOT just an agent that mimics the creator's/brand's/education company's voice.
- It must produce **substance and content of genuine value — not fluff.**
- It's instructed to **learn from and use the client's own best practices, formats, and data.**
- **Not open source.** The goal is to see how effective Risala is for Sawa's clients and for people who want to try it.

## Why it fits the brand
Lands both primary lanes simultaneously: **building with AI (vibe coding)** + **email/newsletters**. The most on-brand challenge Zak could run.
