# Voice Sample — Risalla Challenge / Experiments (2026-06-02)

> Authored by Zak (grammar-cleaned, voice untouched). Stored as a voice sample + the basis for voice-learning entry #3. Resolves canonical spelling: **Risalla** (رسالة).

My co-founder told me you can't build an agent that writes and grows newsletters for creator-preneurs (influencers who want to make money outside of partnerships) and education companies.

So I told him, hell yeah I can…

(while I know little about building AI agents)

So I decided to take this challenge public, and I hope you can help me make the case for building this agent and show him that I actually will :)

To start, let's give this agent a name. We'll call him/it/her Risalla (رسالة), the Arabic word for "a letter."

Our friend Risalla has a couple of important jobs:

- Write emails and newsletters (welcome flows, launch emails, direct response, you name it)
- Research hot topics for these newsletters, and back them with data
- Analyze email data (open rates, CTRs, click-to-open rates…)

Last week, I ran experiments to see how effective Risalla is at mimicking and writing in someone's voice. Here's the breakdown.

Methodology: run the same prompt (write about X topic for this newsletter about {context}), then change or add one variable at a time.

Variables:
1. A content pack with one creator's transcripts, longs and shorts
2. A skill file with instructions on writing frameworks (hero's journey, AIDA, PAS…)
3. Anti-AI rules, a set of instructions for making the writing sound less like AI

And the baseline: the AI writing on the topic, without any variable or training.

Experiment 1 - Content pack vs baseline
Result: the content pack beats the baseline but still has obvious AI patterns.

Experiment 2 - Skill file vs baseline
Result: the skill file beats the baseline but sounds over-engineered.

Experiment 3 - Anti-AI vs baseline
Result: Anti-AI clearly makes the writing better, but it doesn't sound like the creator.

Experiment 4 - (Anti-AI + content pack) vs baseline
Result: far better writing than all of the above.

Experiment 5 - (Anti-AI + content pack + skill file) vs baseline
Result: the writing isn't better than Experiment 4, despite adding the skill.

So for round 1, I settled on the version with the content pack + anti-AI rules.

I'll share more on round 2 in the next few posts, so keep an eye out.
