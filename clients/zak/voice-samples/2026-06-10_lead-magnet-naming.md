# Voice Sample — Lead Magnet (newsletter growth + lead-magnet agent) — 2026-06-10

> Authored by Zak (grammar-cleaned, voice intact). Wednesday lead-magnet pillar. Stored for the voice loop (entry #4). The featured asset (lead-magnet naming best-practices) exists, derived from the agent.

We grew a creator's newsletter from 11,000 to 20,000 subs in less than 3 months, without using ads.

Here's some context behind this: we're working with an influencer who's investing in growing his subscriber list and sending weekly value to them.

His biggest constraint: he doesn't want to spend a dime on ads to grow the newsletter, and would rather channel his thousands of social media followers to it organically.

We couldn't do partnerships with other newsletters just yet, as the list was pretty inactive.

So we opted to use lead magnets to turn these followers into email subscribers.

We were able to churn out one lead magnet almost every other week, which drove the newsletter's growth. And here's the best part: we didn't manually create any of them.

We put together a specific agent that does the job for us. Here's how it works:

**1. It figures out who the creator is.**
We hand it a social media link and their paid product. It reads their profile, studies their content, and pieces together who they are, who their audience is, what their audience struggles with most, and what they're selling. Then it shows us what it found and double-checks the information is right before moving on.

**2. It picks the right problem to solve.**
A good lead magnet solves one small, painful problem really well, and that problem makes the audience realize they need the bigger paid product. The agent lines up three solid options and lets us choose which one to build around.

**3. It chooses the format.**
Cheat sheet, quiz, or email series. It makes a real case for each one based on the audience and the problem, then recommends the best fit. We decide.

**4. It names it.**
It writes three different title options, each with a different angle (the dream outcome, the frustration it removes, or the specific method). We pick.

**5. It builds the outline.**
This is the skeleton: every section, every point, and the call-to-action that bridges into the paid product. We approve the blueprint before any real writing happens.

**6. It runs a quality check.**
Before writing a single word of the final piece, it grades the outline against a strict scorecard. If it's weak, it fixes it.

**7. It writes the content.**
It writes in the creator's actual voice, not generic AI slop. It shows us a sample first so we can react, then finishes the whole thing once we're happy.

**8. It designs and delivers.**
It pulls the creator's brand colors and fonts, drops everything into a clean design, and hands back a finished, ready-to-use file in both HTML and PDF.

This process takes less than 30 minutes per lead magnet. Imagine how many it can churn out per day.

But truth be told, what helped us get high uptake on these lead magnets was the way we named them. People aren't going to give a damn about a cheat sheet or list if it doesn't name their pain, trigger their curiosity, and incentivize action immediately.
