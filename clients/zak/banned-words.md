# Banned Words & Moves — Zak

The `scrub-ai-tells` skill enforces this list. Add to it whenever a tell slips through.

## Banned words / phrases (AI tells)
- delve, leverage, unlock, harness, elevate, navigate, tapestry, testament, realm, landscape, foster, robust, seamless, game-changer, supercharge, dive in, deep dive, embark, journey (as filler), ever-evolving, fast-paced, cutting-edge, paradigm, synergy, holistic, empower, unleash

## Banned openers
- "In today's world / in a world where / in the age of…"
- "Let's face it / Let's be honest…" (as filler)
- "Picture this:" (overused)
- "Hi, I'm [name], I have X years of experience…"

## Banned structures
- Rule-of-three for rhythm ("faster, cleaner, smarter")
- Antithesis cliché: "It's not just X, it's Y" / "X isn't about Y, it's about Z"
- Forced symmetry / perfectly balanced clauses
- Em-dash overuse (max 1 per post)
- Perfectly parallel bullet lists when prose is more natural
- Empty hedges: "It's worth noting," "Needless to say," "At the end of the day," "Without a doubt"

## Banned CTAs
- "What do you think? Comment below!"
- "Drop a 🙌 if you agree"
- "Like and share!"
- Any vague "DM me" without a specific reason/word
