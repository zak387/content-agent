# Content Strategy — Zak

## Cadence
Five posts per week, one per weekday. Run via `/run-content-week`.

## Primary lanes (center of gravity)
1. **Email marketing & funnels** — subject lines, sequences, CTAs, conversion, deliverability.
2. **Vibe coding / building with AI** — shipping products with AI agents, building in public.

## Adjacent themes (in service of the two above, used sparingly)
- Creator economy
- Creator monetization
- Info products

## Weekly pillar map
| Day | Pillar | Notes |
|-----|--------|-------|
| Mon | Re-intro (first Monday of cycle) → then Challenge announcement | Sets up the cycle; challenge creates Mon→Fri open loop |
| Tue | Viral post / adaptation | Borrow a trending *mechanic*, not the words |
| Wed | Lead magnet | Give real value; route to claim |
| Thu | Personal story | One true story → one earned lesson |
| Fri | Challenge update / output | Honest results of the week |

## Cycle structure
- A "cycle" = one challenge run, 4 weeks (30 days), optionally extendable to 60 days.
- Cycle Week 1 Monday = re-introduction.
- Every following Monday = challenge announcement/checkpoint.
- Every Friday = the challenge's weekly output/result.

## Voice north star
Warm mentor / builder-in-public. Generous, transparent, specific. Wins AND failures.
