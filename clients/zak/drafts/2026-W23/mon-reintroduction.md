## Post

LINKEDIN!!! I've been super inactive for the past few months, but now I'm so back and want to re-introduce myself.

My name is Zakaria Laajily, born and raised in Casablanca, Morocco.

I started in influencer marketing back in 2023, and I learned lots from the likes of Augmentum Media and Saulderson Media, to whom I'm forever grateful.

That experience taught me a lot about business and entrepreneurship, and it ignited a spark in me to do stuff for more than just myself, to create outsized outcomes and build something with lasting impact and real profit for me, my loved ones, and the broader community.

So after an aggregate of 18 months with some of the best agencies in the UK, I decided to pivot, to grab the bull by the horns and start my own business.

My first business, MPWR was an agency that helped creators build their own digital products, from creation to scaling. For about a year, I learned client management and how to deal with different personalities and stakeholders and that led to me helping a client make 5 figures consistently from his course.

But more notably, it taught me a lot about myself and the business I want to build, one where people need to have skin in the game. A will, plan, and accountability to keep trying and failing.

Which led me to my second business, a startup called Allii.
The idea was to solve legacy problems in the Influencer Marketing industry.
The big hairy problem was that the infrastructure was super decentralized, but not in a good way. Smart, capable marketers were stuck on admin, excel entry, email follow-ups, CRM management, which is not optimal for agencies or brands.

I set out to solve it with AI as most people didn't know how to harness it, so I saw an opportunity there, and people were genuinely excited. A real problem that needed solving ASAP for agencies wanting to scale and brands wanting to bring it in-house.

But eventually I shut it down, because I transparently didn't have the technical/AI capabilities to solve it. That was my biggest learning.

A couple of months forward, I'm now working on Sawa with my brother and partner Ziad Hassan, where we're growing and writing newsletters for creator-preneurs, education and ecommerce companies.
