Example: building the newsletter agent (Risala) at Sawa for almost two months — trial and error, tests, mapping processes, brainstorming with Claude Code, reading tons of docs — running in circles, no significant progress. Underneath: never thought of myself as a product/technical guy. Proof = previous startup Allii; biggest bottleneck was product, and believing "I'm not technical" stopped me moving forward. Now adopting "as you walk, the way appears," letting go of the psychological shackles in my head. Don't need to be technical or a product expert to build a product — I need to try, take steps, assess, pause, improve. A lot of what we fear is in our heads; had we started walking, we'd have more clarity and confidence.
