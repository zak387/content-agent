Another week, another gem from Sharran.

It goes like this: "The gap between talented people who build something real and talented people who don't is the willingness to act before having all the answers."

Boy, isn't it so good!!

It made me think of our business. For context, we started as a digital product studio for knowledge creators, and we realized it's not the game we want to be in for the long term.

So we shifted gears toward doing email for creator-preneurs. But we found ourselves in a situation where we don't have all the answers.

We don't have the answer to whether writing newsletters for creators is the best thing to do.

We don't have the answer to how to get product-market fit.

We don't have the answer to whether writing newsletters for startups would make more sense.

Nor do we have the answer to whether doing launch campaigns is the best service we can offer right now.

But here's what this quote made us realize: we have to get our hands dirty more often. Stop thinking, start doing. Seek out the answers by testing and experimenting, instead of asking for advice and waiting around. Get the answers ourselves.

So that's the plan. We're going to try all of it. Write newsletters for creators, do email marketing for startups, run the launches, and let experimentation lead instead of overthinking.

Sometimes that feels liberating, even exciting.

Sometimes it feels daunting and tiring.

But that's the game.

Not one of certainty, but one of figuring things out as we go.

We're just two Moroccan guys trying to make it in business.
