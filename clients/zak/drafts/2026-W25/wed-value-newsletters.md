# Wednesday — Value Post: "Creators are sleeping on newsletters" (2026-W25)

**Pillar:** Value / lead-magnet (educational, no gated asset — CTA is "go start a newsletter")
**Status:** APPROVED (authored by Zak; grammar-cleaned, bullet 3 landing added)
**Quote check:** "financially irresponsible not to have a newsletter" verified as **Nathan May** (via Growth in Reverse). Zak's paraphrase is fair.

Creators are sleeping on newsletters!

I know it firsthand. I was putting together a list of creators in the B2B, AI, and tech niches, and then it hit me.

Only 30% of that list had an email newsletter, and roughly 10% were actively growing and monetizing it.

Nathan May puts it cleanly: if you're a founder, it's financially irresponsible not to have a newsletter.

And the same goes for creators. If you're a creator, it's financially irresponsible not to have a newsletter.

So is it that some creators need this more than others?

I'd argue creators in any niche or segment need their own list, from fashion to education to cybersecurity.

So why is it important, and more relevant today?

- You own your audience. This is pretty urgent right now, because the social media algorithms have crowned content as king. Whether a piece comes from a big influencer or a faceless theme page, it doesn't matter. To the Metas and ByteDances of the world, you don't matter, only how "good" your content is. I hate to say it, but that's the biggest, most obvious threat to you as a creator.

- Selling services and making more money. Put simply, if you're still over-reliant on paid partnerships through social as your primary revenue driver, you're in a tough place. The tricky part is it doesn't feel like it in the short term. You feel secure because the money is coming in and the sponsors are pouring in. But if you want to think like a proper entrepreneur (which you are), you can't put all your eggs in one basket.

- It compounds into an asset. This is the big one for me. As I was writing this, I kept thinking about the MrBeasts and Emma Chamberlains of the world building real creator-led businesses. So why haven't we seen more creator-led newsletter and media businesses? It's surprisingly right up their alley, way more than launching a CPG brand or a SaaS. A newsletter is the one asset a creator fully owns, and it compounds every single week. That lane is wide open, and the creators who move first will own it.

What's the one key takeaway from all of this?

If you're a creator, or you know one (send this to them), here's what I'd urge you to do:

- Go on beehiiv or Kit > start a free trial > write an email like you're texting a friend > let your followers know you want to send it to them > give them a form to sign up to your list.

That's it. The momentum compounds from there.
