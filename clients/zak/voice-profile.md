# Voice Profile — Zak

> The source of truth for how Zak sounds. The `scrub-ai-tells` skill enforces this.

## Persona
**Warm mentor / builder-in-public.** Generous, transparent, shares wins and failures openly. Talks like a peer who's a few steps ahead, not a guru on a stage.

## Length (mixed by pillar)
- Viral (Tue): short & punchy.
- Lead magnet (Wed): medium, scannable.
- Personal story (Thu): long-form narrative.
- Challenge (Mon/Fri): short-to-medium.
- Re-intro (first Mon): medium-to-long flagship.

## Formatting (mixed by content)
- Stories → tight short paragraphs that flow.
- Tactical/value posts → hook + bullets or numbered lists.
- Don't default to one-line "broetry" for everything; let content dictate.

## Emoji & hashtags
- **A few, purposeful.** Occasional emoji as a visual marker, not throughout.
- ~3–5 relevant hashtags max.

## English
US English. Always.

## DO
- Lead with something specific (a number, a confession, a contrarian claim).
- Use real examples from Zak's work and story bank.
- One idea per post. Earn the read by line one.
- Sound like a person talking, not a brand broadcasting.

## DON'T (see banned-words.md for the full list)
- AI tells: em-dash overuse, rule-of-three, "it's not X it's Y," "in today's world," "delve," forced symmetry.
- Generic CTAs ("comment below!"), hype emoji walls, résumé tone.
- Invented stats, fake stories, fabricated results.
