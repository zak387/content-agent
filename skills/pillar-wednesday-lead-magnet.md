# Skill: Wednesday — Lead Magnet (value-driven give)

## Purpose
Write a value-driven post that gives something genuinely useful away (a newsletter bank, subject-line tips, a CTA framework, a swipe file) and routes interested readers to claim it.

## Input
- `clients/zak/lead-magnets.md` (the inventory of assets Zak has/wants to give).
- `voice-profile.md`, lanes from `content-strategy.md`.

## Output
- Draft → `clients/zak/drafts/<week>/wed-lead-magnet.md`.
- Includes the give itself (or a strong teaser of it) + the claim mechanism (comment-to-DM, link, "comment WORD").
- Medium form, hook + scannable value (often bullets/numbered).

## Process
1. **Stage 1 — Pick the magnet + angle.** Choose a lead magnet from the inventory (or propose one to create). Define the specific value and who it's for. → CHECKPOINT.
2. **Stage 2 — Format.** Decide: full value in-post (teach it) vs teaser + gated claim. Propose structure: hook → the promise → 3–5 concrete value points → the claim CTA. → CHECKPOINT.
3. **Stage 3 — Draft.** Deliver REAL, usable value — not a content-free ad for the magnet. Every tip should be something they could act on today. → CHECKPOINT.
4. **Stage 4 — Hooks + scrub.** Run `hooks.md` then `scrub-ai-tells.md`. Confirm the CTA is specific (not "DM me!"). → CHECKPOINT.

## Banned moves
- No empty teasers ("I have an amazing resource, comment to get it") with zero value in the post itself.
- No invented testimonials, download counts, or results for the magnet.
- Don't promise a magnet that doesn't exist / Zak can't deliver — confirm it's real first.
- No generic "comment below" CTA — use one clear, specific action.

## Failure modes
- `lead-magnets.md` is empty → ask Zak what asset to feature, or offer to outline a new lightweight magnet (e.g. a 10-subject-line swipe file) for him to approve before writing the post.
- The magnet isn't actually built yet → flag it; either write the post as a "building this, want it?" validation post (clearly framed) or pick a different magnet.

## Negative examples
- ❌ "I created the ULTIMATE guide to email marketing 🔥 Comment 'GUIDE' and I'll send it!" — zero in-post value, hype emoji, generic.
- ✅ (shape) "3 subject-line patterns that beat my control last month: [pattern 1 w/ example] [pattern 2] [pattern 3]. Want the full 25-line swipe file? Comment 'SWIPE'." — teaches in-post, specific CTA.
