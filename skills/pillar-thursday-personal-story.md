# Skill: Thursday — Personal Story

## Purpose
Tell one true story from Zak's life/journey — where he grew up, a business he dove into, a person he learned from, a startup/entrepreneurship lesson — that lands an insight relevant to his audience.

## Input
- `clients/zak/story-bank.md` (the source of truth — stories must come from here).
- `voice-profile.md`.

## Output
- Draft → `clients/zak/drafts/<week>/thu-personal-story.md`.
- Long-form, narrative. Tight paragraphs that flow. Warm, builder-in-public.

## Process
1. **Stage 1 — Pick the story + the point.** Choose ONE story from the bank and the single insight it earns. A story without a point is a diary entry. → CHECKPOINT.
2. **Stage 2 — Format.** Structure: in-scene hook (drop us into the moment) → what happened → the turn → the lesson → how it connects to the reader's world. → CHECKPOINT.
3. **Stage 3 — Draft.** Use concrete sensory/specific detail from the bank. One story, one point. Don't moralize too early. → CHECKPOINT (structured feedback).
4. **Stage 4 — Hooks + scrub.** Run `hooks.md` (confession/curiosity angles shine here) then `scrub-ai-tells.md`. → CHECKPOINT.

## Banned moves
- NEVER invent or embellish a story, a detail, a person, or an outcome. Trace everything to `story-bank.md`.
- No fake vulnerability or trauma-for-engagement.
- Don't cram two stories into one post — pick one.
- No tidy fake epiphany the bank doesn't support.
- Don't end with a generic "What's your story? Comment below."

## Failure modes
- The chosen story is thin in the bank (`[NEEDS MORE]`) → ask Zak 1–2 specific follow-up questions to fill the detail BEFORE drafting. Never paper over gaps with invention.
- `story-bank.md` is `[PENDING]`/empty → STOP. This pillar cannot run without it. Run the tastemaker interview.

## Negative examples
- ❌ "Growing up, I always knew I wanted to be an entrepreneur. Every setback taught me resilience." — generic, no specific scene, could be anyone.
- ❌ Inventing "the day my first startup failed I sat in my car and cried" when that's not in the bank — fabrication.
- ✅ (shape) Open mid-scene with a real detail from the bank ("The Stripe dashboard said $0. Day 40 of the launch."), tell what happened, earn one lesson.
