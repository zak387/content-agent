# Skill: Monday — Re-Introduction (first Monday of a cycle)

## Purpose
Write a re-introduction post that tells new and existing followers who Zak is, what he's about, and what to expect from his content going forward.

## Input
- `clients/zak/profile.md`, `story-bank.md`, `voice-profile.md`, `content-strategy.md`.
- Optional: a specific angle Zak wants to lead with this cycle.

## Output
- A draft saved to `clients/zak/drafts/<week>/mon-reintroduction.md`.
- Medium-to-long form (this is a flagship post). Tight paragraphs, warm.

## Process
1. **Stage 1 — Angle.** Propose 2–3 re-intro angles (e.g. "from X background to building Y," "what I post about and why you should care," "the bet I'm making"). → CHECKPOINT.
2. **Stage 2 — Format.** Propose a structure: hook → who I am in one line → the journey in 2–3 beats → what I'm building now → what I'll post about (the pillars) → an invitation to follow/connect. → CHECKPOINT.
3. **Stage 3 — Draft.** Write it. Pull every biographical fact from `profile.md`/`story-bank.md`. Land on the dual lane: email/funnels + building with AI. → CHECKPOINT (structured feedback).
4. **Stage 4 — Hooks + scrub.** Run `hooks.md` then `scrub-ai-tells.md`. → CHECKPOINT (final).

## Banned moves
- No résumé dump ("I have 7 years of experience in…"). Show, don't list.
- No invented credentials, no story not in the bank.
- Don't promise content cadence the strategy doesn't commit to.
- No humblebrag-as-vulnerability ("My biggest weakness is I care too much").

## Failure modes
- `story-bank.md` is empty/`[PENDING]` → STOP and run the tastemaker interview first. A re-intro without real story is the one post you cannot fake. Tell the operator.

## Negative examples
- ❌ "In today's fast-paced digital world, I'm passionate about helping people grow." — generic, says nothing, AI-tell opener.
- ❌ "Hi, I'm Zak. I have extensive experience in marketing and technology and I'm excited to share my journey with you all!" — résumé tone, zero specificity, no hook.
- ✅ (shape) "Three years ago I was [specific situation from bank]. Today I'm building [specific thing]. Here's what I learned in between — and what I'll be posting about." — specific, earns the read.
