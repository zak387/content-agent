# Skill: Hooks (First-Line Generator)

## Purpose
Generate 3 scroll-stopping first lines (hooks) for a drafted post. On LinkedIn the first 1–2 lines decide whether anyone reads the rest — this is the equivalent of an email subject line.

## Input
- A finished draft body.
- The pillar (viral, story, lead magnet, etc.) and `clients/<client>/voice-profile.md`.

## Output
- 3 distinct hook options, each ≤ 2 lines, labeled by angle (e.g. "contrarian," "curiosity gap," "specific result").
- A one-line recommendation of which to use and why.

## Process
1. Identify the single most interesting thing in the post (a number, a tension, a confession, a contrarian claim).
2. Write 3 hooks using DIFFERENT angles:
   - **Specific result / number** — "I sent 14 cold emails. 9 replied."
   - **Contrarian / pattern interrupt** — "Stop A/B testing subject lines."
   - **Curiosity gap / open loop** — "The funnel that doubled my conversions had one ugly page."
   - **Confession / vulnerability** (great for Thursday) — "I shipped a product nobody wanted. Twice."
3. Keep them in-voice (warm, builder-in-public — not clickbait-hype).
4. Make sure line 1 works even before "…see more" expands.

## Banned moves
- No clickbait the post can't pay off ("This ONE trick…", "You won't believe…").
- No question hooks that are answerable with a shrug ("Ever wondered about email?").
- No "In today's world" / generic openers.
- Don't bury the hook — line 1 must carry weight on its own.

## Failure modes
- The post has no clear "most interesting thing" → that's a signal the DRAFT is weak; flag back to the draft stage rather than dressing up a flat post with a hook.
