# Skill: Deepen Brain Dump (Socratic)

## Purpose
Turn a raw brain dump into a richer, more specific RAW file through Socratic questioning — BEFORE any drafting. The job here is to interrogate, not to write.

## Input
- The operator's brain dump + the topic/pillar.
- `clients/<client>/` reference files (to ground, never to invent).

## Output
- A raw material file: `clients/<client>/drafts/<week>/<day>-<pillar>.raw.md`, accumulating the operator's deepened answers in HIS OWN WORDS (verbatim, unpolished).
- NOT a finished post. Drafting is a separate, later step.

## Process
1. **Do not draft.** Read the brain dump and find the vague spots, the unsupported claims, the missing stories/numbers, and the contradictions.
2. **Ask Socratic questions in rounds.** Probe for:
   - Specificity (a concrete moment, not a generalization)
   - Stories (what actually happened, who, when)
   - Numbers (how long, how much, what result)
   - Tension/contradiction (where the operator's own claim fights itself — push on it)
   - The honest stakes (the real fear, the real cost, named plainly)
3. **Reject vague answers.** If an answer is general, ask one sharper follow-up. Don't advance until it's specific.
4. **Append answers verbatim** to the `.raw.md` file. Preserve the operator's phrasing; do not smooth it into LinkedIn voice.
5. **Only when the raw file is genuinely rich**, move to drafting (separately) — and even then, shape his words, don't replace them.

## Banned moves
- Don't write a finished/polished post from a thin brain dump.
- Don't paraphrase the operator's words into generic voice — preserve verbatim.
- Don't accept vague answers and move on.
- Don't invent specifics, numbers, or stories to fill gaps. Extract them from the operator.
- Don't ask everything at once and stop — go in rounds, follow the thread.

## Failure modes
- Operator gives vague answers → one concrete follow-up per gap; hold the line until specific.
- Still thin after probing → say plainly "this isn't ready to draft yet — here's what's missing," rather than papering over it.
