# Skill: Tastemaker Interview

## Purpose
Interview the operator to capture their taste, voice, personal story, and background, then write it into `clients/<client>/voice-profile.md`, `profile.md`, and `story-bank.md`. Run once at onboarding; re-run to refresh.

## Input
- The client name and folder.
- Live conversation with the operator (open-ended answers).
- Any raw materials the operator drops in `clients/<client>/voice-samples/` (past posts, voice notes, transcripts).

## Output
- `clients/<client>/profile.md` — bio, background, what they're building, audience, offers.
- `clients/<client>/voice-profile.md` — how they sound, with rules + DO/DON'T examples.
- `clients/<client>/story-bank.md` — reusable stories, lessons, people, businesses, results.
- Each fact tagged so later skills can cite it. Nothing invented.

## Process
Ask in themed rounds. Keep it conversational — one round at a time, follow up on anything thin. Prefer the operator's exact words; capture phrasings verbatim where they're distinctive.

**Round 1 — Identity & what you're doing now**
1. One line: who are you and what do you do?
2. What are you building / working on right now?
3. Who is your audience on LinkedIn — who are you trying to reach, and what do they want?
4. What do you sell or plan to sell? (offers, products, services)
5. What do you want to be known for in 12 months?

**Round 2 — Background & story (feeds story-bank)**
6. Where did you grow up? What shaped you?
7. What businesses have you started or worked in? What happened with each?
8. Who did you learn from — mentors, founders, people you studied?
9. What's a failure or hard lesson from your startup/entrepreneurship journey?
10. What's a win you're proud of — with real numbers if you have them?
11. What contrarian belief do you hold about your field?

**Round 3 — Voice & taste (feeds voice-profile)**
12. Show me 2–3 posts (yours or others') you wish you'd written. Why each?
13. What words/phrases do you use a lot? Any signature sign-offs or openers?
14. What instantly makes a post feel fake or cringe to you?
15. How formal/casual are you? Do you swear? Use slang? Tell jokes?
16. Read one of your voice notes/DMs — how do you talk when you're NOT performing?

**Round 4 — Content operations**
17. What's the active 30-day (or 4-week / 60-day) challenge for the current cycle?
18. What lead magnets do you have or want to give away?
19. What topics are off-limits? Any clients/people you can't name?

After each round, reflect back what you heard in 2–3 lines and confirm before writing files.

## Banned moves
- Do NOT invent answers or fill gaps with plausible-sounding biography. Empty > fabricated.
- Do NOT flatten the operator's phrasing into generic LinkedIn-speak. Preserve their actual words.
- Do NOT ask all 19 questions in one wall. Round by round.
- Do NOT overwrite an existing populated profile without confirming.

## Failure modes
- Operator gives thin/short answers → ask ONE concrete follow-up per gap, then record what you have and mark the section `[NEEDS MORE]` rather than padding.
- Operator unavailable mid-interview → save partial progress, mark unfilled sections `[PENDING]`, and note in CLAUDE.md status.
- Conflicting info across rounds → surface the conflict to the operator, don't silently pick one.
