# Skill: Monday — Challenge Announcement (subsequent Mondays)

## Purpose
Announce the 30-day / 4-week (optionally 60-day) challenge Zak is committing to publicly this cycle. Creates the open loop that Friday's update pays off.

## Input
- The challenge for this cycle (from operator or `clients/zak/challenges/`).
- `voice-profile.md`, `content-strategy.md`.

## Output
- Draft → `clients/zak/drafts/<week>/mon-challenge.md`.
- Also append the challenge definition to `clients/zak/challenges/<challenge-name>.md` (after approval) so Friday updates have a source of truth.
- Short-to-medium form, high commitment energy.

## Process
1. **Stage 1 — Angle.** Confirm the challenge, the stakes, the deadline, and the public promise. Propose how to frame it (the bet, the constraint, the "watch me try"). → CHECKPOINT.
2. **Stage 2 — Format.** Structure: hook (the bold commitment) → why this challenge → the rules/constraints → what success looks like → invite people to follow along. → CHECKPOINT.
3. **Stage 3 — Draft.** Make the commitment concrete and measurable (so Friday can report against it). → CHECKPOINT.
4. **Stage 4 — Hooks + scrub.** Run `hooks.md` then `scrub-ai-tells.md`. → CHECKPOINT.

## Banned moves
- No vague challenge ("I'll try to post more"). Must be measurable with a deadline.
- Don't promise results that aren't in Zak's control (e.g. "I'll hit 10k followers") — frame around actions/inputs he controls.
- No fake urgency or manufactured stakes.

## Failure modes
- No challenge defined for the cycle → STOP and ask the operator: "What's the challenge this month, what's the measurable outcome, and what's the deadline?" Don't invent one.

## Negative examples
- ❌ "This month I'm challenging myself to be more consistent and grow my brand. Let's see how it goes!" — unmeasurable, no stakes, no hook.
- ✅ (shape) "For the next 30 days I'm [specific measurable action] to [specific outcome]. No skipping. I'll post the results every Friday — wins and faceplants." — measurable, public, sets up Friday.
