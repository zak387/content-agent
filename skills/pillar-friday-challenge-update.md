# Skill: Friday — Challenge Update / Output of the Week

## Purpose
Report the real results of the week's challenge (announced Monday) — what got produced, what worked, what flopped. Closes the open loop. Honest > impressive.

## Input
- The active challenge from `clients/zak/challenges/<challenge>.md`.
- The REAL results/output from the operator (numbers, screenshots described, what shipped). This is operator-supplied each week.
- `voice-profile.md`.

## Output
- Draft → `clients/zak/drafts/<week>/fri-challenge-update.md`.
- Medium form. Builder-in-public energy — wins and faceplants both.
- Append the week's result to `clients/zak/challenges/<challenge>.md`.

## Process
1. **Stage 1 — Get the real numbers + angle.** Confirm with the operator: what was the goal, what actually happened, the honest delta. Pick the angle (a win, a flop-with-lesson, a surprising result). → CHECKPOINT.
2. **Stage 2 — Format.** Structure: hook (the result, stated plainly) → recap the challenge in one line → what happened (specifics) → what I learned → what's next. → CHECKPOINT.
3. **Stage 3 — Draft.** Lead with the real outcome, good or bad. Specificity sells; vagueness reads as hiding. → CHECKPOINT.
4. **Stage 4 — Hooks + scrub.** Run `hooks.md` (specific-result angle is strong) then `scrub-ai-tells.md`. → CHECKPOINT.

## Banned moves
- NEVER invent or inflate results. If the week was a flop, the post is about the flop. Honesty is the brand.
- Don't spin a failure into a fake win.
- Don't report results without the operator confirming the actual numbers/output.
- No "and that's why consistency matters!" filler lesson — the lesson must be earned by what actually happened.

## Failure modes
- Operator hasn't provided real results yet → STOP and ask: "What actually happened this week — the numbers, what shipped, what flopped?" Do NOT draft a results post with placeholder/invented outcomes.
- Results are genuinely "nothing happened / I skipped it" → that's a legitimate, honest post (the discipline of showing up even when you fail). Offer to write it that way.

## Negative examples
- ❌ "Week 1 of my challenge was a huge success! Learned so much and can't wait for more 🚀" — vague, no numbers, reads as hiding bad results.
- ❌ Reporting "+300 subscribers" when the operator hasn't confirmed any number — fabrication, instant trust-killer.
- ✅ (shape) "Goal was 50 cold emails. I sent 31. Got 4 replies, 1 call, 0 sales. Here's what I'd change." — specific, honest, sets up next week.
