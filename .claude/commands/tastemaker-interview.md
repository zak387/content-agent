# Tastemaker Interview

Run `skills/00-tastemaker-interview.md` for client `$1` (default `zak`).

- Go round by round (4 rounds). Don't dump all questions at once.
- Reflect back what you heard after each round and confirm before writing.
- Write results into `clients/<client>/profile.md`, `voice-profile.md`, and `story-bank.md`.
- Capture the operator's exact phrasings where distinctive. Never invent answers — mark gaps `[NEEDS MORE]` or `[PENDING]`.
- When done, update the status section in `CLAUDE.md` and append a line to `DECISIONS.md`.
